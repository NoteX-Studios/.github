## Our Services:

- ### Asset on Demand(AOD):
    We offer some services to improve and make game development for you much easier. We do it either by providing you stock or customized assets.

- ### Game on Demand(GOD):
    GOD (Game On Demand) is a service by NoteX Studios that turns your game ideas into fully playable games. You don’t need coding skills or a team, just your idea. We handle everything from development to trailer creation, and you get full ownership to publish and sell the game under your own name.
